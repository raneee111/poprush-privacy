Pop Rush

Pop Rush is a fast, colorful arcade reflex game for Android — pop the called color, keep the combo alive, and play across Time Attack, Survival, Instinct, a 100-stage Journey campaign, and local split-screen Versus. It's free, with optional online leaderboards and Squads. Questions or support: rlstudio.dev@gmail.com.

The rest of this page is the app's Privacy Policy. Effective date: 7 August 2026 · App: Pop Rush (com.rlinteractive.poprush) · Contact: rlstudio.dev@gmail.com

Summary

The core game requires no sign-in, and the game data you create (scores, progress, settings) stays on your device. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob; to serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners process a device advertising identifier and ad-interaction data. Advertising is the only thing that sends data off your device without you choosing to.

Two things are off unless you turn them on:

  • Gameplay analytics — the app includes Google Analytics for Firebase (a Google service). Analytics collection ships disabled and no gameplay event is sent to Google until you explicitly allow it in the app. See Analytics.
  • Online Features — leaderboards and Squads. Turning them on signs you in with Google Play Games and connects to our backend; nothing networks until you explicitly enable them in-app.

Pop Rush is currently released on Android only. Where this policy mentions iOS, it describes what will apply if and when an iOS version ships.

What the app stores on your device

  • Game progress & settings — best scores, campaign stars/unlocks, coins, and your preferences (sound, language). Stored locally via the OS (Unity PlayerPrefs).
  • Local gameplay event log — the app writes anonymous gameplay events (e.g. a mode started, a stage cleared or failed, a run ended, an ad offered or completed) as one line each to a file in the app's own private storage. This file contains no personal information and is never uploaded — we have no server that receives it. It is separate from the Firebase analytics described under Analytics, which sends a smaller subset of the same events to Google, and only if you allow it.

This on-device data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your real name, email, contacts, or photos, and the app requests no location permission. (If you enable Online Features, you sign in with Google Play Games and your Google Play Games display name becomes your online name — see Online features & Squads. Google may derive an approximate, country/region-level location from your IP address for ads and, if you enable it, analytics — see the sections below.)
  • We do not sell your data, and there are no purchases in the app.
  • We do not put your name, your email address, your Google Play Games player ID, your online profile ID, or any free text you type (such as your display name) into analytics events. Analytics events carry only a fixed set of gameplay values — see Analytics.
  • Apart from the Google services described in this policy (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play Games Services and Google Play's in-app review prompt) and our online backend (PlayFab), the app contains no other analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking SDK.
  • We do not use cookies or web tracking.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Pop Rush includes Google Analytics for Firebase, an analytics service provided by Google. We use it to understand how the game behaves and how it is played: whether it runs stably, how far players get through the campaign, which stages are too hard, how long a session lasts, and which modes and features actually get used.

It is off until you allow it. The app ships with Firebase analytics collection disabled at the operating-system level, so nothing is collected even before the game's own code runs. Collection is enabled only after you give explicit consent to analytics through an in-app control; the app then records that choice and tells the Google SDK about it. Consent you give to ads is deliberately not reused as consent to analytics — they are separate purposes and we treat them separately. If your version of the app has never asked you for analytics consent, analytics collection is off and no gameplay event is sent to Google.

What is sent, if you allow it. Only a fixed, short list of gameplay events:

  • mode_start / mode_end — a run started or finished. Values: which mode (journey, time_attack, survival, instinct, versus), and, where they apply to that mode, the campaign stage number, the chosen difficulty (easy/normal/hard), whether it was the first game on this install, how the run ended (cleared, completed, times_up, out_of_lives, red_tap, abandoned), the score, and the duration in seconds.
  • stage_clear / stage_fail — a campaign stage was cleared or failed, with the stage number.
  • ad_clicked, reward_completed, interstitial_shown — you chose to open an optional rewarded ad, finished watching one, or were shown an interstitial. The first two also carry the place in the game it was offered (continue, energy, skip or double_coins); interstitial_shown carries no values at all.

That is the whole list. Everything else the game records stays in the on-device log described above and is never sent.

What we do not send. These events contain no email address, no real name, no Google Play Games player ID, no online profile ID, no display name, and no free text you type. They carry only the bounded gameplay values listed above.

What Google may process automatically. When analytics collection is enabled, the Google SDK also processes technical information on its own, independently of the events we send. According to Google, this includes an app-instance identifier (a random ID for one installation of the app), a device advertising identifier, device model and operating system, app version, session counts and duration, and an approximate, country/region-level location derived from your IP address. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase and Firebase data collection. We have configured the app so that analytics data is not used to personalise ads.

Turning it off. Because collection only ever begins after you explicitly allow it, declining — or simply never being asked — means nothing is collected. If you have allowed it, you can withdraw that permission using the same in-app control, which stops collection immediately and keeps it off on later launches. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Pop Rush ▸ Storage, also resets the app-instance identifier and removes the local log.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pop Rush is ad-supported. Ads are served by Google AdMob:

  • The app shows rewarded ads (optional, player-initiated: refill campaign energy, continue a stage, or double earned coins — you only watch one if you tap it) and interstitial ads (occasionally between arcade-mode games — never during active gameplay).
  • AdMob and its partners may collect and process a device advertising identifier and related ad-interaction / diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, per Google's Advertising Policies and the Google Privacy Policy. This data is shared with Google.
  • If an iOS version ships, the app will request App Tracking Transparency permission there; declining still shows (non-personalized) ads.
  • In the app's default state — ads on, analytics off, Online Features off — this advertising data is the only data that leaves your device.

Online features & Squads (optional, off by default)

Pop Rush includes optional Online Features: global leaderboards and private Squads (small invite-code groups with their own weekly and all-time leaderboards). They are disabled by default and make no network connections until you explicitly turn them on via the in-app consent dialog. Turning them on is optional and reversible — turning Online Features off stops further syncing.

When you enable Online Features, you sign in with Google Play Games Services (a Google service) and the game connects to our backend (PlayFab, a Microsoft game-services platform). The following syncs to game servers — matching the in-app "What's shared" screen:

  • Google Play Games sign-in — enabling Online Features authenticates you with your Google account through Google Play Games Services. We receive your Google Play Games player ID and display name and use a one-time authorization code to link that account to your online profile, so your online progress follows you across devices. Google Play Games is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your online profile — identified by a random identifier generated by the app and linked to your Google Play Games account. We do not request your email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • Your display name — taken from your Google Play Games profile name; you can change it in-app at any time. It is visible to other players on leaderboards and to your squad members.
  • Your best scores per mode — visible on leaderboards you participate in.
  • Squad membership — the squad invite code and your membership/scores, visible to the members of that squad.

PlayFab processes this data on our behalf; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. None of the data in this section is shared with anyone unless you turn Online Features on.

Deleting your data

Most Pop Rush data lives only on your device: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Pop Rush ▸ Storage, permanently removes your scores, progress, settings, and the local analytics file.

If you turned on Online Features, an online profile (a random app-generated identifier linked to your Google Play Games account, your display name, your best scores, and any squad membership) is stored on our backend (PlayFab). To delete that online data and the associated account:

  • In the app: turn Online Features off in Settings to stop all syncing, then
  • Request deletion: email rlstudio.dev@gmail.com from the address you contact us with, or include your in-game nickname, and we will delete your online profile and associated data within 30 days.

There are no purchases and no payment data to delete.
